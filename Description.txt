[size=3][b]Description[/b][/size]

Quartermaster is a profile-driven configurator and build pipeline for Windrose. A small desktop GUI lets you mix and match gameplay tweaks across items, loot, trade, cooldowns, building, lighting, navigation, ship handling, inventory, equipment, jewelry, FX and audio, then bakes them into a single "_P.pak" you drop into the "~mods" folder. Every value is calculated against a live vanilla snapshot extracted directly from the game's pak, so multipliers really do what they say.





[size=4][b]Stack sizes[/b][/size]
[color=#b6b6b6]Per-item or global multiplier/absolute caps; weapons, armor and other unstackable items stay vanilla unless you explicitly promote them.[/color]

[size=4][b]Bonfire radius[/b][/size]
[color=#b6b6b6]1.0x to 5.0x slider for the building-center influence sphere (vanilla 50 m / 30 m). Optionally upload a custom audio file (WAV/MP3/FLAC/OGG) to replace the bonfire ambience with adjustable volume.[/color]

[img width=800]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/themelektaus/windrose-quartermaster-mod/refs/heads/main/Media/nexusmods.com/Bonfire-radius.png[/img]





[size=4][b]Cooldowns, crops & crafting[/b][/size]
[color=#b6b6b6]Bidirectional sliders (0.1x to 3.0x) on eight cooldown families: Elixir, Medicine, Spell of Return, Ship Repair Kit, Boar Whistle, Ship Summon, Ranged Weapon Reload, Ship Cannon Reload. The Ship Cannons card pairs its reload slider with a cannon-range slider (1.0x to 5.0x) and a cannon-damage slider (1.0x to 10.0x); all three are player-only, so enemy and NPC ships keep their vanilla reload, range and damage. A Soul Eater - Soul Harvest card scales the Souldrinker greatsword's [F] special (Base + Advanced tiers): cooldown plus life-drain damage and radius (0.1x to 10.0x each). A Ship Boarding card loosens grappling on your own ships (Brig, Cutter, Frigate, Ketch): max boarding range (1.0x to 5.0x), aim forgiveness (1.0x to 5.0x), hull-angle tolerance (1.0x to 2.0x) and max closing speed (1.0x to 3.0x). The same tab also retunes crop grow times and crafting/processing durations (furnace, kiln, tannery, mill, trade outpost).[/color]

[img width=800]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/themelektaus/windrose-quartermaster-mod/refs/heads/main/Media/nexusmods.com/Cooldowns%2C-crops-%26-crafting.png[/img]





[size=4][b]Crop overlap[/b][/size]
[color=#b6b6b6]Shrinks the planting-collision footprint of every crop so seeds can be placed much closer together (0.1x to 1.0x slider, default 0.4x = -60% footprint). Lower means more overlap; 1.0x leaves vanilla spacing untouched.[/color]

[img width=800]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/themelektaus/windrose-quartermaster-mod/refs/heads/main/Media/nexusmods.com/Crop-overlap.png[/img]





[size=4][b]Pickup radius[/b][/size]
[color=#b6b6b6]Free slider from 1.0x to 10.0x for the auto-pickup magnet range (vanilla 4 m). Optional 1.0x to 4.0x multiplier (right in the Pickup radius card) that widens the collection range for floating sea-loot and the interaction zones of the Brig, Frigate and Ketch, so you scoop up drifting cargo from further out.[/color]

[img width=800]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/themelektaus/windrose-quartermaster-mod/refs/heads/main/Media/nexusmods.com/Pickup-radius.png[/img]





[size=4][b]Fast-travel bells & signal fires[/b][/size]
[color=#b6b6b6]Raise the placement caps (vanilla 10 / 3). An optional toggle allows inland fast-travel placement, so bells can be placed anywhere - not just near the coast.[/color]

[img width=800]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/themelektaus/windrose-quartermaster-mod/refs/heads/main/Media/nexusmods.com/Fast-travel-bells-%26-signal-fires.png[/img]





[size=4][b]Pickaxe range[/b][/size]
[color=#b6b6b6]1.0x to 3.0x slider that scales the trace radius on every pickaxe tier so you can hit nodes from slightly further away.[/color]

[img width=800]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/themelektaus/windrose-quartermaster-mod/refs/heads/main/Media/nexusmods.com/Pickaxe-range.png[/img]





[size=4][b]Loot tables[/b][/size]
[color=#b6b6b6]Per-category Min/Max multipliers (mobs, plants, chests, fishing, ...) leave everything else alone.[/color]

[img width=800]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/themelektaus/windrose-quartermaster-mod/refs/heads/main/Media/nexusmods.com/Loot-tables.png[/img]





[size=4][b]On death[/b][/size]
[color=#b6b6b6]Two independent toggles. Keep death loot from despawning - a persistent storage bag spawns at the death spot instead of the timed posthumous container, so your dropped items stay in the world (land and at sea). Keep status effects - food, elixir and comfort buffs survive death instead of being wiped, while transient debuffs (damage-over-time, stagger, wet, ...) still clear.[/color]

[img width=800]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/themelektaus/windrose-quartermaster-mod/refs/heads/main/Media/nexusmods.com/On-death.png[/img]





[size=4][b]Player stats[/b][/size]
[color=#b6b6b6]Scale the player's base Health and Stamina above vanilla with two independent 1.0x to 10.0x sliders (vanilla 320 HP / 150 stamina). Health grows the health bar; Stamina grows the bar and speeds up how fast it refills. Leave either at 1.0x to keep that stat vanilla.[/color]

[img width=800]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/themelektaus/windrose-quartermaster-mod/refs/heads/main/Media/nexusmods.com/Player-stats.png[/img]





[size=4][b]XP rewards[/b][/size]
[color=#b6b6b6]Two independent base multipliers (0.1x to 10x) on the XP you earn - one for every quest reward (faction / main / side / local-event), one for every POI treasure-chest reward. A dedicated XP rewards tab additionally lets you pin an individual quest's or chest's experience with a per-entry override; a row left at 1x just follows its overall multiplier. Only the reward amounts scale - the level curve itself, and entries that grant no vanilla XP, are left untouched.[/color]

[img width=800]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/themelektaus/windrose-quartermaster-mod/refs/heads/main/Media/nexusmods.com/XP-reward.png[/img]





[size=4][b]Level rewards[/b][/size]
[color=#b6b6b6]Scale the talent and stat points granted on every level-up. Two multipliers (1x to 10x) raise each level's reward; since some vanilla level-ups give only 0-1 talent points, a boost ADDS the multiplier on those lean levels instead of scaling (at 3x: 0->3, 1->4), while richer levels scale normally (2->6, 3->9). A dedicated Level rewards tab also lets you pin an exact talent / stat reward per individual level. The XP curve and the level-1 starting row are never touched. New characters get the bigger rewards as they level; the Characters tab retro-fits the matching point totals onto a character already in your save (with an automatic backup first).[/color]

[img width=800]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/themelektaus/windrose-quartermaster-mod/refs/heads/main/Media/nexusmods.com/Level-rewards.png[/img]





[size=4][b]XP for Kills[/b][/size]
[color=#b6b6b6]Grant flat XP on every enemy kill - awarded through the game's own level-up logic, so it sticks in your save exactly like a quest reward (points, level-ups and the on-screen notification, not just a number). A slider on the Basic tab sets the default XP for any enemy (0 to 100, where 0 = off / vanilla, no XP on kill); a dedicated XP for Kills tab lists every vanilla enemy type (generated from the game pak) so you can pin a per-enemy amount. A broad keyword like "Mob_Boar" covers all its variants through substring matching, and a more specific row wins.[/color]

[img width=800]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/themelektaus/windrose-quartermaster-mod/refs/heads/main/Media/nexusmods.com/XP-for-Kills.png[/img]





[size=4][b]NPC Spawns[/b][/size]
[color=#b6b6b6]Global multipliers for enemy spawn counts (1.0x to 4.0x on each spawner's Min/Max) and respawn time (0.1x to 2.0x), applied to NPC / enemy spawners only - resource, chest and mineral-node spawners are left alone. Optionally also speed up rare / boss timers. A dedicated NPC Spawns tab lets you override individual spawners (respawn minutes, count min/max) on top of the global values.[/color]

[img width=800]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/themelektaus/windrose-quartermaster-mod/refs/heads/main/Media/nexusmods.com/NPC-Spawns.png[/img]





[size=4][b]Sea Shanties[/b][/size]
[color=#b6b6b6]Replace any of the 10 vanilla shanty slots with your own audio (WAV/MP3/FLAC/OGG, auto-transcoded to BinkAudio), add extra shanties alongside the vanilla 10, and tune the volume of each track individually. Exclude single slots if you only want the vanilla tracks you like.[/color]

[img width=800]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/themelektaus/windrose-quartermaster-mod/refs/heads/main/Media/nexusmods.com/Sea-Shanties.png[/img]





[size=4][b]Keep Shanties Playing[/b][/size]
[color=#b6b6b6]Vanilla stops the crew shanty the moment you step away from the ship's wheel - turn this on and it keeps playing after you leave the helm, so you can roam the deck without the music cutting out. At the helm, B still starts and stops it as usual. Singleplayer only, not designed for multiplayer.[/color]

[img width=800]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/themelektaus/windrose-quartermaster-mod/refs/heads/main/Media/nexusmods.com/Keep-Shanties-Playing.png[/img]





[size=4][b]Building stability[/b][/size]
[color=#b6b6b6]Structures hold longer cantilevers and taller towers without collapsing.[/color]

[img width=800]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/themelektaus/windrose-quartermaster-mod/refs/heads/main/Media/nexusmods.com/Minimap-range.png[/img]





[size=4][b]No smoke[/b][/size]
[color=#b6b6b6]Hide the smoke and flame Niagara FX on campfires/bonfires, furnaces or kilns.[/color]

[img width=800]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/themelektaus/windrose-quartermaster-mod/refs/heads/main/Media/nexusmods.com/No-smoke.png[/img]

[img width=800]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/themelektaus/windrose-quartermaster-mod/refs/heads/main/Media/nexusmods.com/Deposit-visuals.png[/img]

[img width=800]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/themelektaus/windrose-quartermaster-mod/refs/heads/main/Media/nexusmods.com/Lighting.png[/img]





[size=4][b]Equipment slots[/b][/size]

[size=4][b]Inventory & Storage Size[/b][/size]
[color=#b6b6b6]Three independent multipliers on inventory capacity. Player Inventory scales the base 16-slot grid (1x to 10x). Chest Storage scales the capacity of every chest type (small, medium, large, ship cargo). Backpack Slots scales the extra slots each backpack tier adds to your inventory (0.25x to 10x, e.g. a Tier 3 backpack gives +16 vanilla - at 3x that becomes +48). New characters and chests use the scaled values automatically; the Characters tab retro-fits the player inventory onto an existing save (with an automatic backup first).[/color]

[img width=800]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/themelektaus/windrose-quartermaster-mod/refs/heads/main/Media/nexusmods.com/_Inventory-and-Storage-Size.png[/img]





[size=4][b]Jewelry Stats[/b][/size]

Every value is scaled from the live vanilla motor curve, so nothing else about the ships changes.[/color]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]1. [/b]Download and run "Quartermaster.exe" (Windows) or "Quartermaster.Web" (Linux / Steam Deck). On first start it auto-extracts the vanilla item JSONs and icons from your Windrose install - takes 30-90 seconds.[/*]
[*][b]2. [/b]Create a profile, pick the tweaks you want, hit [b]Build[/b]. The resulting "_P.pak" lands directly in "<Steam>\steamapps\common\Windrose\R5\Content\Paks\~mods\".[/*]
[*][b]3. [/b]Launch the game (or restart the server). For multiplayer the same pak has to live in the server's "~mods" folder.[/*]
[*][b]4. [/b]To switch between configurations, build a new profile and let the Mods tab recycle the old pak - only one Quartermaster build should live in "~mods" at a time.[/*]
[/list]


[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Windrose[/b] (any version that supports the "~mods" pak loading convention).[/*]
[*][b]Windows:[/b] Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime - preinstalled on Windows 11 and recent Windows 10 builds.[/*]
[*][b]Linux / Steam Deck:[/b] any modern browser for the local web UI. Wine is auto-detected from your Steam library (Proton or GE-Proton works out of the box); no extra install needed if you already use Steam.[/*]
[*]No UE4SS, no SML, no other mod loader required.[/*]
[/list]


[size=4][b]Report an issue[/b][/size]
There is a "Report" button that bundles your active profile, the latest build log and your "~mods" folder listing into a single archive so problems can be diagnosed quickly.
