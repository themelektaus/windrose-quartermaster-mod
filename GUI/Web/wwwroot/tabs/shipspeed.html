Overall

Base multiplier applied to every ship curve below whose own slider is at 1x. Per-curve overrides win when set.

1.0x

Vanilla = 1.0x. Range 0.1x - 10x.

Per-ship motor speed

Each curve scales a ship's throttle→thrust response (its motor force, which drives acceleration and top speed). A slider at 1x follows the Overall multiplier; any other value pins that curve to its own scale. "Player" is the ship you sail; "AI" the enemy/NPC version; "Service" the slow docking/maneuver curve; "BlackBeard" / "Brethren" the faction boss ships.
