Add Extra Ship Shanties

Adds entirely new shanties to the rotation alongside the 10 vanilla ones (instead of replacing them - that lives on the regular Ship Music tab). Tracks are appended to every ship-type DataAsset (Cutter, Brig, Ketch, Frigate), so the crew picks from the bigger pool on any vessel.

Each track needs a stable identifier (the Track Key; letters/digits/underscore, no spaces) and an audio file. Quartermaster transcodes the audio to 44.1 kHz stereo WAV, Bink-encodes it and packs a fresh SoundWave plus four SoundCue clones (one per ship size + crew-only variant) into the mod-pak.

Heads-up: this writes patched copies of the four DA_*_AudioParams DataAssets into the mod-pak. Combining this with another mod that touches the same DataAssets (e.g. Extra Sea Shanties) is not supported - last pak loaded wins.
