Custom Sea Shanties

Configure the sea-shanties the crew sings on board. Three things live here:

  • Replace any of the 10 vanilla shanties with your own audio. A replacement applies to all ship sizes automatically.
  • Exclude a vanilla shanty from the rotation so the crew stops picking it. Excluding does not delete your replacement - re-including restores it instantly.
  • Add entirely new shanties beyond the vanilla 10. Added tracks are appended to every ship-type DataAsset (Cutter, Brig, Ketch, Frigate), so the crew picks from the bigger pool on any vessel.

Drop an audio file - Quartermaster transcodes it to a 44.1 kHz stereo WAV (if needed), encodes it to Bink Audio and packs the result into a SoundWave at build time. No UE5 Editor cook needed.

Accepted formats: .wav, .mp3, .ogg, .flac, .m4a, .aac, .opus. Non-WAV files are transcoded by ffmpeg.exe (~190 MB) which is staged during first-time setup. If you skipped that step, re-open setup to enable non-WAV uploads.

Vanilla Shanty Slots
